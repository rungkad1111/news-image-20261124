## Stories used (all reported 24 November 2026)

2. **Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine** — an overnight Russian strike on an apartment building killed 2 and wounded 40, including four children.
3. **Abu Dhabi peace talks** — US envoys Kushner and Witkoff concluded talks and headed to Moscow and Dublin; Lavrov rejected European calls for an immediate truce.
4. **Gaza** — Israeli airstrikes killed Hamas internal security chief Naeem Abu Nahel and six others, including two police officers.

7. **DR Congo** — heavy fighting near a hospital in Ituri trapped Ebola responders for over 24 hours; Congo also halts cobalt exports from 30 November.
8. **Senegal / Atlantic** — about 175 migrants unaccounted for after contact was lost with a Europe-bound boat that left Dakar two weeks earlier.
9. **Bangladesh** — garment workers clashed with police in Gazipur over pay; tear gas fired and dozens of factories shut.
10. **Israel** — Eisenkot's Yashar party clinched its first coalition agreement, with Lieberman's Israel Our Home, in the bid to form a government.
11. **US economy** — consumer confidence jumped to 103.3, the highest in nearly two years.
12. **China** — extended the suspension of its ban on US-bound gallium, germanium and antimony exports to 27 January 2027.
13. **Germany / Ukraine** — Germany became the first country to gain access to Ukraine's Delta battlefield management system.
14. **Iran / US** — Vance and Rubio met Qatar's prime minister at the White House as Iran vowed to use all its tools against the US naval blockade.
